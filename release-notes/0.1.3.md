Clearer panning and tidier page tabs.

- **Pan with Space.** Hold Space over the drawing and a grab hand shows that dragging moves around the page. While you type text, Space still types a space.
- **Page tabs.** The tabs no longer show scrollbars that cut off the page names. With many pages, scroll the tabs with the mouse wheel or trackpad; the current page's tab stays in view, the insert-page button stays beside the tabs, and the zoom controls on the right are no longer squeezed out.
