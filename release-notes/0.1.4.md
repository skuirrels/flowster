Faster opening and text that lines up.

- **Text lines up with its shapes.** Words in table cells no longer overlap or spread apart, labels stay centred in their boxes, and rotated swimlane labels sit in their bands instead of being cut off. Centred and right-aligned text also stays in place in SVG exports.
- **Large drawings open faster.** A big drawing opens in about half the time it used to, and the window shows "Opening…" with the file's name while it loads.
