Drawings open fitted to the window, and an easier-to-read shortcuts list.

- **Drawings open fitted.** A new or opened drawing now fills the window, even after you zoomed into the previous one. Before, it kept the last drawing's zoom and could appear small in a corner.
- **Keyboard shortcuts at a glance.** *Help › Keyboard shortcuts* opens a wide window with the shortcuts in three columns, so you no longer need to scroll.
