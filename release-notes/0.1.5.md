Page setup, and opening drawings from Finder.

- **Page orientation and size.** The new Design menu, and the Inspector when nothing is selected, set a page to portrait or landscape and to Letter, Legal, Tabloid, A5, A4, A3, A2 or a custom size. *Fit to drawing* shrinks the page to fit the shapes.
- **Auto size.** As in Visio, the page grows by whole sheets of paper as shapes reach past its edges, and shrinks again when they move back. Turn it off in the Design menu. New drawings start with it on.
- **Open with Flowster.** Double-clicking a Visio drawing, stencil or template in Finder or Explorer now opens it in Flowster. On a Mac, *File › Settings…* can make Flowster the default app for those files.
