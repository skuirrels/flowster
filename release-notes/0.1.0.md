The first preview of Flowster: a diagramming app that opens and saves Visio files.

- **Open and save Visio drawings.** Opens .vsdx, .vssx and .vstx (macro-enabled ones too) and saves .vsdx. Save writes back to the file you opened; AutoRecover keeps your changes if Flowster closes unexpectedly.
- **Draw.** Shapes from the Basic, Flowchart and Swimlane stencils, text, lines and connectors that stay glued to shapes. Double-click empty space to add a shape by name.
- **Arrange.** Snap to the grid and to other shapes with smart guides, align, distribute, rotate, flip, group, and bring to front or send to back. Copy, paste, duplicate (or Alt+drag) and copy style.
- **Pages.** Add, rename, hide and delete pages from the tabs at the bottom.
- **Find your way.** Rulers, grid, a minimap, zoom to selection (Shift+2) and a command palette (Ctrl+K) with every command.
- **Export and print.** PDF with the fonts embedded, PNG and SVG, and printing.

This is an early preview: keep a copy of drawings you care about before saving over them. Flowster checks for updates when it starts, and from Help ▸ Check for updates.
